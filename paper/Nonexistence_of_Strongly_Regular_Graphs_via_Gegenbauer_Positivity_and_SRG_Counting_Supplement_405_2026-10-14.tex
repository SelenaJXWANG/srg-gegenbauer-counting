\section{Complete rational certificate for 405}
\label{app:405-explicit}
This supplement specifies all $174$ sources and their coefficients for
$(405,132,63,33)$, including every value of all $107$ weight functions.
It also gives formulas that recover every coefficient of the contradiction
from this printed data. No numerical optimizer is part of this specification.


A graph in the tables is specified by $(h,b)$: it has vertex set
$\{0,\ldots,h-1\}$ and an edge $ij$, $i<j$, exactly when bit
$j(j-1)/2+i$ of the nonnegative integer $b$ is one. The symbol $y_F$
means $2^{\binom h2}N_F/(405)_h$, where $h=|F|$, and is unchanged by relabelling $F$.
The source index $i$ runs from $1$ to $174$. The printed coefficient
$w_i$ is already reduced; no further row normalization is applied.

% Insert after the supplement section heading, before the certificate inventory.
Let $\Gamma$ be a hypothetical strongly regular graph with parameters
$(405,132,63,33)$. For a labelled graph $F$, let $N_F$ count its
injective induced embeddings into $\Gamma$. Write
$(405)_h=\prod_{j=0}^{h-1}(405-j)$, with $(405)_0=1$, and
$[m]=\{0,\ldots,m-1\}$. For a graph $H$ on $[m]$,
$\mathcal N_H(b)$ is the neighbourhood of $b$, and $H_C$ adds one
labelled vertex adjacent precisely to $C\subseteq[m]$.
A binary profile has coordinate $j$ equal to one precisely when
its vertex is adjacent to selected vertex $j$.
The SRG identities give the positive semidefinite matrix
\[
 G=\frac{9I+3\operatorname{Adj}(\Gamma)-J}{8},\qquad
 \operatorname{rank}G=30.
\]
Here $J$ is the all-one matrix. Thus $G$ is the Gram matrix of unit vectors $p_x\in\mathbb R^{30}$.

\subsection*{Exact reconstruction formulas}
For $i\le67$ set $R_i(y)=-y_{F_i}$ using Table~\ref{tab:405-countdata}.
For $i\ge68$, take $H_i$, $\ell_i$ and $f_i$ from
Table~\ref{tab:405-kerneldata}. An array in that table is read row by
row in binary lexicographic profile order, with coordinate $0$ first.
Thus the first entry is $f_i(0,\ldots,0)$ and the last is
$f_i(1,\ldots,1)$. All $2^{|H_i|}$ entries are present.

For an $m$-vertex anchor $H$ and profiles $\sigma,\tau\in\{0,1\}^m$, put
\begin{align*}
 G_H&=(9I+3\operatorname{Adj}(H)-J)/8,
 &b_\sigma&=(3\sigma-\mathbf1)/8,\\
 h_\sigma&=1-b_\sigma^TG_H^{-1}b_\sigma,
 &z_{\sigma\tau}(e)&=(3e-1)/8-b_\sigma^TG_H^{-1}b_\tau,\\
 D&=30-m,
 &(\kappa_0,\kappa_1,\kappa_2)(z,h,h')
   &=\left(1,z,\frac{Dz^2-hh'}{D-1}\right).
\end{align*}
Here $H_\sigma$ adds one labelled vertex with profile $\sigma$,
and $H_{\sigma,\tau;e}$ adds two labelled vertices with those profiles
and edge indicator $e$ between them. Every selected $G_H$ is positive
definite. For row $i\ge68$, take $H=H_i$ and $m=|H_i|$ in these
definitions; thus $h_\sigma$, $z_{\sigma\tau}(e)$ and $D$ belong to
that row's reference graph. Define the row explicitly by
\begin{align}
 R_i(y)={}&-\sum_\sigma f_i(\sigma)^2h_\sigma^{\ell_i}y_{(H_i)_\sigma}
 \notag\\
 &-\frac{404-m}{2^{m+1}}
 \sum_{\sigma,\tau}\sum_{e=0}^1
 f_i(\sigma)f_i(\tau)
 \kappa_{\ell_i}\bigl(z_{\sigma\tau}(e),h_\sigma,h_\tau\bigr)
 y_{(H_i)_{\sigma,\tau;e}}.
 \label{eq:405-printed-rows}
\end{align}
The convention $h^0=1$ is used. Isomorphic extensions are combined
with their full multiplicities. Formula~\eqref{eq:405-printed-rows}
is the negative normalized kernel energy, so $R_i(y)\le0$.
% Insert after eq:405-printed-rows, replacing its existing one-sentence
% assertion that the negative normalized energy is nonpositive if desired.
For a fixed anchor, let $q_x\in\mathbb R^D$, $D=30-m$, be the
orthogonal residuals of $p_x$ for vertices outside the anchor.
With arbitrary real weights $a_x$,
the sums of $a_xa_y\kappa_0$ and $a_xa_y\kappa_1$ are
$(\sum_xa_x)^2$ and $\|\sum_xa_xq_x\|^2$, respectively, while
\[
 \sum_{x,y}a_xa_y\kappa_2(q_x^Tq_y,\|q_x\|^2,\|q_y\|^2)
 =\frac{D}{D-1}\left\|\sum_xa_x
 \left(q_xq_x^T-\frac{\|q_x\|^2}{D}I_D\right)\right\|_F^2\ge0.
\]
Here $\|\cdot\|_F$ is the Frobenius norm.
Take $a_x=f_i(\sigma(x))$ and sum over all labelled embeddings of
the anchor. Multiplication by the positive factor
$2^{\binom{m+1}{2}}/(405)_{m+1}$ gives the negative of
\eqref{eq:405-printed-rows}. Hence $R_i(y)\le0$.


Here is a finite recursion specifying all ordinary counting substitutions.
Let $\mathcal C$ contain one graph of each isomorphism type on at most
seven vertices with minimum degree at least three. It has $173$ elements.
Introduce $x_C=y_C$ for $C\in\mathcal C$. Graph arguments are understood
up to isomorphism: set $\mathcal T(K_1)=1$ and
$\mathcal T(F)=x_C$ whenever $F\cong C\in\mathcal C$.
Fix a labelled representative for each type; for definiteness one may
choose the representative with the least integer edge mask.
For any other type $F$ on at least two vertices, choose a vertex of
minimum degree, delete it to obtain an $m$-vertex graph $H$, and let
$B\subseteq[m]$ be its neighbour set. Then $|B|\le2$, and set
\begin{equation}
 \mathcal T(F)=\frac{2^m e(H,B)}{405-m}\mathcal T(H)
 -\sum_{B\subsetneq B'\subseteq[m]}\mathcal T(H_{B'}),
 \label{eq:405-printed-recursion}
\end{equation}
where
\[
 e(H,B)=
 \begin{cases}
 405-m,&B=\varnothing,\\
 132-\deg_H(b),&B=\{b\},\\
 63-|\mathcal N_H(b)\cap\mathcal N_H(c)|,
       &B=\{b,c\},\ b\sim_Hc,\\
 33-|\mathcal N_H(b)\cap\mathcal N_H(c)|,
       &B=\{b,c\},\ b\not\sim_Hc.
 \end{cases}
\]
Choose the least labelled vertex to break ties, and retain the increasing
order of the surviving labels after deletion. The recursion terminates
because each term has fewer
vertices, or the same number of vertices and more edges. It specifies
$\mathcal T(F)$ using only the ordinary one-vertex counting equations.
The saved independent reconstruction checks all these equations and the
identity rows for the free types. Induction through the recursion then
shows that any choice of representatives gives the same affine expression
in the isomorphism-type variables $x_C$.

Substitute $y_F=\mathcal T(F)$ in every printed source and collect terms:
\begin{equation}
 \sum_{i=1}^{174}w_iR_i\bigl(\mathcal T\bigr)
   =c_0+\sum_{C\in\mathcal C}c_Cx_C.
 \label{eq:405-printed-coefficients}
\end{equation}
This equality defines the rational coefficients completely from the tables
and formulas above. Put $U_C=2^{\binom{|C|}{2}}$ and
$E=-\sum_{c_C<0}c_CU_C$. Exact substitution gives
\begin{equation}
 c_0>\frac{570}{10^{10}},\qquad
 0\le E<\frac{11}{10^{10}}.
 \label{eq:405-printed-rationalbounds}
\end{equation}
These are strict rational comparisons, not rounded decimal estimates.
The original nonnegative counts and empty-set extension equations imply
$0\le x_C\le U_C$. Thus the fully specified certificate is
\begin{align}
 0\;&\ge\sum_{i=1}^{174}w_iR_i\bigl(\mathcal T\bigr)
      +\sum_{c_C\ge0}c_C(-x_C)
      +\sum_{c_C<0}(-c_C)(x_C-U_C)\notag\\
   &=c_0-E>\frac{559}{10^{10}}>0.
 \label{eq:405-printed-contradiction}
\end{align}
All $174$ printed $w_i$ are strictly positive. The direction arrays and
rational coefficients below were generated from the exact inputs and
checked against them entry by entry. In particular, the three
second-degree rows are $i=75,76,85$, all with $(m,b)=(5,222)$,
namely $K_{2,3}$ with bipartition $\{0,1\}\cup\{2,3,4\}$.

\clearpage\section{The all-degree five-point relaxation for 405}\label{app:405-tail}
The same parameters admit a rational feasible point for the five-point
real-count relaxation at \emph{every} Gegenbauer degree. Temporarily put
$q_F=N_F/(405|\operatorname{Aut}F|)$ and choose
\[
 \begin{array}{c|rrrr}
 F&K_5\setminus P_3&K_5\setminus2K_2&K_5\setminus K_2&K_5\\
 q_F&988060&134777&287429&68038
 \end{array}
\]
The first graph has two adjacent edges deleted.  Exact elimination
reconstructs all $52$ nonnegative counts and verifies the ordinary
extension equations.  All profile matrices with at most three
reference vertices are PSD at every degree.  The accompanying
all-degree verifier checks the $66$ necessary low-degree matrices
by exact Schur elimination; the remaining degrees follow from
the rational tail estimate described below.  This is a feasible
point of a real relaxation, not a graph construction.

Here $P_\ell^{(D)}$ is the Gegenbauer polynomial for $S^{D-1}$,
normalized by $P_\ell^{(D)}(1)=1$. At every degree the profile matrices
use the extension coefficients of Section~S1 and the homogeneous kernel
$\kappa_\ell(z,h,h')=(hh')^{\ell/2}P_\ell^{(D)}(z/\sqrt{hh'})$.
For $q\in\mathbb Z_{\ge0}$ and $D>2$, put
\[
 B_{q,D}(s)=\sum_{j=0}^q\binom qj s^{q-j}(1-s)^j
 \prod_{a=1}^j\frac{2a-1}{D+2a-3},
\]
with the empty product equal to one.  The normalized Gegenbauer
integral representation \cite[18.10.4]{DLMF} gives
$|P_\ell^{(D)}(t)|\le B_{q,D}(s)$ whenever $t^2\le s<1$
and $\ell\ge2q$.  Indeed,
$P_\ell^{(D)}(t)=\mathbb E(t+i\sqrt{1-t^2}Z)^\ell$,
where $Z$ is the first coordinate of a uniform point on
$S^{D-2}$; take absolute values and use
$\mathbb E Z^{2j}=\prod_{a=1}^j(2a-1)/(D+2a-3)$.
The expression $t^2+(1-t^2)Z^2$ is at most one and increases with
$t^2$, which proves the bound.
For an anchor with $1\le m\le3$, undo the common positive normalization
of Section~S1, which preserves positive semidefiniteness. Fix its
reference graph $H$, put $D=30-m$, and enumerate its profiles as
$\sigma_1,\ldots,\sigma_{2^m}$ in binary lexicographic order. Put
$n_i=N_{H_{\sigma_i}}$ and $c_{ij}(e)=N_{H_{\sigma_i,\sigma_j;e}}$.
Swapping the two new labels gives $c_{ij}(e)=c_{ji}(e)$.
These nonnegative single-profile and distinct-pair masses obey
$\sum_{j,e}c_{ij}(e)=(404-m)n_i$.
After dividing coordinate $i$ by $h_{\sigma_i}^{\ell/2}$, the diagonal contribution is
$\operatorname{diag}(n_i)$.  For any real column $u=(u_i)_{i=1}^{2^m}$, the absolute quadratic
contribution of all distinct pairs is at most
$(404-m)B_{q,D}(s)\sum_i n_i u_i^2$, by
$2|u_iu_j|\le u_i^2+u_j^2$.  Thus every tail matrix is PSD if
$(404-m)B_{q,D}(s)\le1$.

The exact maximum squared residual angles and sufficient tail
cutoffs for the seven nonempty anchor types are
\[
\begin{array}{c|c|c|c}
H&D&s&\ell\text{ in the tail}\\\hline
K_1&29&3/35&\ell\ge8\\
\overline{K_2}&28&1/8&\ell\ge8\\
K_2&28&9/64&\ell\ge8\\
\overline{K_3}&27&1/4&\ell\ge10\\
K_2\mathbin{\dot\cup}K_1&27&867/4715&\ell\ge10\\
P_3&27&3/11&\ell\ge12\\
K_3&27&4/21&\ell\ge10
\end{array}
\]
All heights are positive, and the displayed tail inequalities are
checked over $\mathbb Q$.  Hence increasing the degree alone
cannot exclude $405$ within this five-point real-count relaxation,
whereas seven points and
degrees at most two suffice.  The sufficiency of the intermediate
six-point level is not determined by these results.
